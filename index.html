Hobbie
MVP Pipeline

Spontaneous activity matching for urban professionals.

The Problem

Urban professionals live near thousands of potential activity partners but still fail to coordinate in real time. Dating apps introduce social friction and performative pressure, while async group platforms create slow, cluttered planning threads that break spontaneous momentum.

The Solution

Hobbie uses an ephemeral, intent-driven matching engine built around what you want to do right now. Rooms are strict 2-4 hour TTL, location discovery uses coarse geohashing with fuzzed coordinates, and interest notifications enforce k-anonymity floors to preserve privacy at low density.

Trust & Safety

Participation requires symmetric face-scan verification for hosts and joiners, producing visible trust badges before engagement. A rolling-average trust score model (not a raw cumulative report counter) is used to prevent coordinated false-reporting loops from unfairly triggering punitive outcomes.
